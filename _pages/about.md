## Research Interest

I work on **efficient LLM inference** across the stack, from algorithms down to serving systems and kernels. Currently:

- **Speculative decoding**: draft model training, draft-side KV cache pruning for long-context reasoning, and task-aware heterogeneous drafters
- **LLM serving systems**: scheduling and speculative decoding in inference engines, GPU kernels and runtimes
- **Efficient agents**: faster context compaction for long-running agents

Previously, I worked on memory-efficient training of deep GNNs for circuit graphs.
